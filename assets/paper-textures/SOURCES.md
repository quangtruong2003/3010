# Paper texture maps

The three 2K color maps are from ambientCG and are released under CC0:

- `paper-fine.webp` — [Paper 001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001), fine ivory stock.
- `paper-fiber.webp` — [Paper 002](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper002), flecked cartonage.
- `paper-crease.webp` — [Paper 003](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper003), lightly creased stock.
- `paper-handmade.webp` — [recycled paper texture photo by frank minjarez](https://www.pexels.com/photo/textured-brown-recycled-paper-background-36119288/), resized to 3072px and converted to WebP for the envelope and letter surface.

The ambientCG color maps were converted to WebP at quality 92; texture detail and dimensions were retained for the web version. The Pexels image is free to use under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
